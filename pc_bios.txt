PC BIOS

Real mode 16-bits 8086 compatible ROM routines available at boot time for a PC since 1983. The working of it is best illustrated by calling them in an assembler.

INT 10h Video

Set video mode:

MOV AH, 0x00
MOV BL, 0x03
INT 0x10
